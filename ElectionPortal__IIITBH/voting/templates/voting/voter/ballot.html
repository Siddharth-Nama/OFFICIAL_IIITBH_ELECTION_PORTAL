How to Vote

 – Instructions
Follow these steps to cast your vote successfully:

✅ Step 1: Review the Position
The current position (e.g., Vice President) is shown at the top of the card.

✅ Step 2: Select One Candidate
You must select only one candidate for the position.

Click the radio button above the candidate's image to select them.

You can view the candidate's Bio by clicking the 🔍 Bio button under their photo.

🔁 Step 3: Reset Selection (if needed)
If you want to change your choice, click the green Reset button on the top-right of the box.

🔄 Step 4: Navigate Between Positions
Use the Next and Previous circular buttons on the sides to move between different positions.

👀 Step 5: Preview Your Ballot
Once all positions are filled, click the green Preview button at the bottom to see your full ballot.

📩 Step 6: Submit Your Vote
After reviewing, click the blue Submit button to finalize your vote.

⚠️ Important: Once submitted, you cannot vote again.
